FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is Eats AI?+
Eats AI is an all-in-one AI nutrition app for iPhone and Android. Snap a photo of any meal to log calories and macros instantly, manage your pantry with expiry alerts, generate recipes from ingredients you already own, plan your week with automatic shopping lists, and get dish recommendations from restaurant menus near you. It comes with a 7-day free trial.
How does the free trial work?+
Every new account gets a 7-day free trial with full access to everything: AI scanning, recipes, meal plans, the works. No commitment: cancel anytime before the trial ends and you won't be charged.
How accurate is the AI photo scanning?+
The AI recognizes thousands of foods and estimates portions from a single photo, and you can always adjust anything before saving. Estimates are for general wellness. If you have allergies or strict medical requirements, always verify ingredients independently.
Can it help me when I'm eating out?+
Yes, and this is where Eats AI is truly one of a kind. It analyzes your goals and taste profile, reads the menus of restaurants near you, and recommends the specific dishes that fit. No more guessing at the table.
Is it available on iPhone and Android?+
Yes. Eats AI is on the App Store and Google Play.
Can it handle my dietary preferences and allergies?+
Yes. Set your goals, dietary style, and allergies once, and every generated recipe and meal plan respects them automatically.
How is Eats AI different from Cal AI or MyFitnessPal?+
Apps like Cal AI focus on photo calorie counting, and MyFitnessPal centers on database food logging. Eats AI covers the whole picture in one app: photo and voice logging, a smart pantry with expiry alerts, recipes generated from what you own, weekly meal plans with shopping lists, and restaurant dish recommendations near you.
How much does it cost after the trial?+
Eats AI is free to download with a 7-day free trial of everything. After that, an affordable subscription unlocks full access. Current pricing is shown in the app and on the App Store and Google Play, and you can cancel anytime in seconds.
Do you sell my data?+
No. We never sell your personal data, and you can delete your account and all your data at any time from inside the app. Details in our Privacy Policy.
