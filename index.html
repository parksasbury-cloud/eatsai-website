How accurate is the AI photo scanning?+
The AI recognizes thousands of foods and estimates portions from a single photo, and you can always adjust anything before saving. Estimates are for general wellness. If you have allergies or strict medical requirements, always verify ingredients independently.
Can it help me when I'm eating out?+
Yes, and this is where Eats AI is truly one of a kind. It analyzes your goals and taste profile, reads the menus of restaurants near you, and recommends the specific dishes that fit. No more guessing at the table.
