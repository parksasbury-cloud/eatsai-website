Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 23, 2026

Welcome to Eats AI, a product of MUPPBURY HOLDINGS LLC ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and services.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information You Provide

When you create an account and use Eats AI, you may provide:

  • Account information (name, email address)
  • Profile information (age, weight, height, dietary preferences, health goals)
  • Food and meal data (photos, descriptions, nutrition logs)
  • Ingredient and pantry inventory
  • Progress photos and health metrics
  • Sign-in information from Google, Apple, or TikTok, if you choose to create your account using one of those services. See Information from Third-Party Sign-In below for exactly what each provider gives us.

Automatically Collected Information

  • Device information (device type, operating system, unique device identifiers)
  • Usage data (features used, interaction with the app, time spent)
  • Location data (if you grant permission, for local ingredient recommendations)

Information from Third-Party Sign-In

You can create an account or sign in using Google, Apple, or TikTok instead of an email address and password. You authenticate directly with that provider, and we never receive or store your password for it. What we receive depends on the provider:

  • Google: your name, your email address, your Google profile picture, and a unique identifier for your Google account
  • Apple: your name, your email address, and a unique identifier for your Apple account. If you use Apple's Hide My Email option, we receive a private relay address instead of your personal one
  • TikTok: a unique account identifier, your display name, and your profile picture, under TikTok's user.info.basic permission

We use this information to create and secure your Eats AI account, to show your name in the app, and to recognize you when you sign in again. As described in the Information Sharing and Disclosure section, we also pass your account identifier to our analytics and crash reporting providers, and on iOS we pass your email address and display name to the provider that manages subscriptions, so that your subscription stays attached to your account.

Whichever sign-in method you use, the account record we create is stored by our authentication provider, Google Firebase.

We do not receive your videos, drafts, followers, direct messages, or viewing activity from any of these services, and we never post, comment, or take any other action on your behalf.

You can revoke Eats AI's access at any time in your provider's account settings. Revoking access does not delete your Eats AI account or the data in it. To delete those, use the delete account option in the app or contact us using the details in the Contact Us section below.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain our services
  • Generate personalized recipe recommendations
  • Analyze nutrition data and provide health insights
  • Track your progress toward health goals
  • Send expiry alerts and notifications (if enabled)
  • Improve our AI models and app functionality
  • Respond to your requests and provide customer support
  • Ensure app security and prevent fraud

3. Third-Party AI Services

Use of Third-Party AI Providers

Eats AI uses third-party AI services to provide features such as food recognition, nutrition analysis, recipe generation, and meal planning assistance. These services are essential to the core functionality of the app.

Data Shared with AI Providers

The app may send limited user-provided content to third-party AI providers only when required to process AI-powered features that you actively request. The data shared is limited to content voluntarily provided by you for feature functionality, such as:

  • Meal or food images uploaded by you
  • Food descriptions or nutrition-related prompts entered by you
  • Ingredient lists used for recipe generation

The app does not share sensitive personal information such as payment information, government identifiers, contacts, or unrelated device data with AI providers.

User Consent for AI Data Processing

Users provide consent before using AI-powered features. Consent is obtained through the onboarding/privacy consent flow and by user-initiated actions such as uploading meal images, scanning food items, or submitting prompts for AI processing. By taking these actions, you acknowledge that the relevant content may be processed by third-party AI services as described in this Privacy Policy.

4. AI and Image Processing

Eats AI uses artificial intelligence to analyze food images and generate nutrition data. When you upload photos:

  • Images are processed by AI services to identify food items and estimate nutrition
  • We may retain images to improve AI accuracy and app functionality
  • Images are stored securely and are not shared publicly
  • You can delete your images at any time through the app

5. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information with:

  • Service Providers: Third-party companies that help us operate our services (authentication and account management, AI processing, cloud storage, analytics)
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, legal process, or to protect rights and safety
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets

We require all third parties to respect the security of your data and treat it in accordance with the law.

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit and at rest
  • Regular security assessments
  • Access controls and authentication
  • Secure cloud infrastructure

However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request copies of your personal data
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data (via app reset or account deletion)
  • Data Portability: Request transfer of your data
  • Object: Object to processing of your data
  • Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent where we rely on it

To exercise these rights, you can use the in-app settings or contact us at the email provided below.

8. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law. You can delete all your data at any time using the "Reset App" feature in Settings.

Information we receive from a third-party sign-in provider, including account identifiers, display name, and profile picture, is retained for the life of your account and is deleted when you delete your account.

9. Children's Privacy

12. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have specific rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • Right to know what personal information is collected, used, shared, or sold

  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights

To exercise these rights, contact us at admin@eatsai.org. We will verify your identity before processing requests.

  • Right to data portability

Our legal basis for processing your data includes: consent, contractual necessity, legal obligations, and legitimate interests.
